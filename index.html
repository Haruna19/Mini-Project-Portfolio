Getting to know Phainon

Phainon, real name is Khaslana, is a 5-star playable character from a free-to-play, turn-based space fantasy RPG developed by HoYoverse, called 'Honkai: Star Rail'. As the deuteragonist of the Aedes Elysiae region, he is a Destruction path user who functions as the "Flame Reaver", a simulated being or "chosen one" trapped in 33 million time loops.
